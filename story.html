One court. One city. A time when words were dangerous.

GERA KEGN tells the cases that pass through a single court — each one a story about justice, fear and courage.

Background

A novel written under the Derg

GERA KEGN began as a novel written during the Derg era — a story in which judges, advocates, clerks and the accused meet inside one courtroom.

In those years, to write about the law was to write about power. Through the court's cases, the novel shows a city living under watch — who speaks, who stays silent, and what silence costs.

Adapted for television, it ran for two seasons and became a drama many still remember today.
