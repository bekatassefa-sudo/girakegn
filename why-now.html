FILE 05WHY NOW

The case was adjourned. It was never closed.

Four reasons GERA KEGN is returning — as a film, and now.

01

The audience never left

Years after the final episode, the episodes are still shared, the characters still quoted, and the question is always the same: when is it coming back?

One generation remembers. Another discovers.

Those who lived through those years know the story rings true. Their children and grandchildren are only now hearing it. The cinema puts both in the same room.

03

Ethiopian cinema is ready

Audiences are returning to cinemas and want their own stories on the big screen. GERA KEGN brings them one they already love and trust.

The audience can produce it

Today an audience doesn't have to just watch. Through crowdfunding, diaspora ambassadors and partners, the people who loved the story can make the film happen.
